PLAIN-LANGUAGE PRIVACY NOTICE

Your camera stays with you.

Community Squats uses your browser to check movement. The service receives small counting records, not a video feed.

What never leaves your device

Camera video and MediaPipe pose landmarks are processed in browser memory and are not uploaded to or stored by Community Squats. Closing the page or camera ends that local processing.

What the service stores

  • Your Twitch numeric user ID and current login name after Twitch sign-in.
  • A short-lived signed-in session and anti-forgery value. Viewer OAuth access and refresh tokens are not retained after identity verification.
  • Server-issued run IDs, calibration standing/squat angles and duration, cumulative rep counts, sequence numbers, elapsed timing, daily totals and automated abuse flags.

Why it is used

The data attaches your daily 100-rep allowance to one Twitch identity, adds accepted reps to the stream's Community Count, prevents accidental duplicates, and helps the broadcaster review obviously impossible submissions.

Services involved

Twitch handles sign-in under its own privacy terms. Your browser downloads the pose library/model from jsDelivr and Google-hosted MediaPipe model storage. The hosting and reverse-proxy provider may process normal connection metadata such as IP address for delivery and security; the Community Squats application database does not intentionally store your IP address.

Retention and deletion

Live participation data remains until you delete it or the operator removes it. While signed in, select Delete my viewer data on the Viewer page to erase your Viewer identity, calibration/runs, flags and contributions; accepted reps are removed from the Community Count. Operational backups may retain deleted records until the operator's backup retention period expires.

Important limitation

Browser-local checks discourage casual cheating but cannot cryptographically prove exercise. This is an entertainment feature with no cash value, prizes, withdrawals or financial rights.
