SCommunity Squats

COMMUNITY GAME RULES

Move honestly. Cause chaos responsibly.

This is a stream interaction experiment: complete locally checked squats, grow the Community Count, and use the result only for entertainment.

How a rep counts

  • Sign in with your own Twitch account and allow camera access on your device.
  • Calibrate by showing a stable standing pose, a comfortable squat depth, and a return to standing.
  • Each rep must complete your calibrated stand → depth → stand cycle while tracking is active.
  • The server accepts only ordered cumulative updates with plausible timing. Network failures can pause a run and require a retry or recalibration.

Limits

Each Viewer may contribute up to 100 accepted reps per channel day. A normal run targets 20 reps; the final run may be shorter when you are near the daily limit. The reset uses the channel's configured timezone.

Fair play and moderation

Do not fabricate requests, automate counts, share sessions, interfere with the service, or try to bypass the daily cap. Impossible-speed activity is flagged. The broadcaster may revoke suspicious or mistaken contributions, which removes those reps from the Community Count and can end that run.

No economic value

Counts are play points only. They cannot be sold, transferred, withdrawn, redeemed for money or relied on as proof of exercise. The operator may reset, change or end the experiment.

Look after yourself

Use a clear area, stay within a comfortable range, and stop if you feel pain, dizziness or unusual discomfort. This software is not medical or fitness advice and does not evaluate whether exercise is safe for you.
